# Share a trail with a friend

## Find a walk before travelling

Open **Trails & GPX** and search for **Southern Ridges Walk** in Singapore. Open the named route and its map, then save the trail while you have internet. The saved line can be reopened offline. Download an available country map before departure if you also need detailed streets or paths offline. A gap between mapped sections stays visible.

## Send the path from a personal walk

1. Open **Saved trails** and select the walk.
2. Tap **Share GPX**, then **Route only**. Choose your friend and preferred messaging app.
3. Your friend opens the attachment in Waystitch, reviews the preview, taps **Save privately**, then **Open trail map**.

**Route only** includes the exact coordinates and any breaks in the recording. The resulting file uses a generic name and leaves out timestamps, altitude and speed. Choose **Full GPX** to include the recorded name, times and readings where available. A mapped trail shared from the discovery screen keeps the map source attribution.

## Send a meeting point

Open a saved trail and tap **Share trail start** to send a single point. Your friend can open the Waystitch link and preview a walking route there. On a trail map with a current GPS fix, **Share my position once** also lets you choose whether to include the fix time. Each pin stays at the location you sent.

## Suggest a public trail

You can walk, save and share your personal tracks without signing in. To suggest adding a public walk, open **Community** on the app’s Home screen. A GitHub account lets you submit a proposal for studio review. Only include public information you're comfortable attaching to your account. [Waystitch community dashboard](https://github.com/nee00ooo/speedometer-map-packs/blob/main/community/README.md)

Southern Ridges Walk is mapped by [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and listed by [Waymarked Trails](https://hiking.waymarkedtrails.org/#route?id=5993965).
